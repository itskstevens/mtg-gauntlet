How to read this

  • Every matchup is a best-of-3 with no sideboarding; the loser of a game plays first in the next.
  • The AI is rule-based. It's good at creature combat, removal and counters, and weak at engines it doesn't model (Affinity cost reduction, Tron lands, flicker loops, Delver flipping). Treat low-trust columns as not meaningful.
  • Burn decks mostly aim burn at your creatures, so the burn matchups are kinder to you than they would be in real life.
  • With this many matches a win rate moves about ±3 points between runs. Differences smaller than that are noise.
