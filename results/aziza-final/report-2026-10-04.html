Cast cheap instants and sorceries that make tokens, copy the best ones with Aziza by tapping three creatures, and let Impact Tremors-style drains turn every token into damage to each opponent. Mob Justice, Boros Charm or a combat swing finishes the game. The deck makes about 28 tokens and 79 drain damage a game, and typically wins around round 9.

Game plan

  1. Turns 1–3: engine. Fast mana (Sol Ring, Chrome Mox, Ancient Tomb, Signets), then a drain piece (Impact Tremors, Warleader's Call or a pinger) and a token maker (Young Pyromancer, Prismari Pianist, Monastery Mentor). Aziza comes down cheap at {R}{W}.
  2. Turns 3–6: go wide. Chain token spells. Once you have three creatures, Aziza copies each one, and every token entering pings the whole table. Battle Hymn, Jeska's Will, Seething Song and Mana Geyser fuel big turns.
  3. Finish. Mob Justice (copied if you can), Boros Charm, Empty the Warrens off a long chain, or a hasty X spell (Tempt with Vengeance, Forth Eorlingas!) into a big attack.

What to watch for

  • Aziza gets removed constantly, about once every game and a half, but the deck keeps winning without her. Don't hold back token spells waiting to copy them.
  • Your losses come early (median round 8): go-wide combat (Jetmir, Arabella), Fynn's poison, and fast combos (Kinnan, Niv-Mizzet). Save Swords and Path for those.
  • Toxic Deluge is the wipe you face most, and indestructible doesn't stop it. Don't overextend into four open black mana.
  • Lifegain is your weakest table-mate (39%): life gain offsets 1-point drains. Mob Justice and Boros Charm close those games.

Rulings to know

The interactions this deck runs into most, with Comprehensive Rules numbers (2026-09-25 edition, from your mtg-rules repo). Tap a ruling to open it.

Copies and casting

Aziza's copies aren't cast.

They don't trigger Guttersnipe, Young Pyromancer, Prismari Pianist or Monastery Mentor, and they don't add to storm. Storm-Kiln Artist's magecraft says "cast or copy", so it does make a Treasure for each copy.

CR 707.10
You can tap summoning-sick creatures for Aziza.

The summoning-sickness rule only stops a creature's own {T} abilities and attacking. Tapping three creatures as part of Aziza's trigger isn't either, so tokens made this turn can pay, the same as with convoke (Wildfire Awakener).

CR 302.6, 702.51a
Copies keep X and modes.

A copied Song of Totentanz, Forth Eorlingas!, Tempt with Vengeance or Grand Crescendo gets the same X for free, and a copied Boros Charm or Jeska's Will keeps its chosen modes.

CR 707.10
Twinning Staff adds one copy to every copy effect.

Aziza makes two copies instead of one, and storm makes one extra.

CR Card text
Storm counts spells cast, not copies.

Empty the Warrens copies itself once per spell cast before it this turn. Aziza's copies from earlier don't add to that count. A copy of Empty the Warrens isn't cast, so it doesn't trigger storm again.

CR 702.40a, 707.10
Return the Favor can copy a triggered ability.

With spree you can pay {1} more to copy Aziza's trigger (another spell copy, if you can tap three more creatures) or an Impact Tremors or Purphoros trigger.

CR 702.172a

Drains and the board

Every creature entering is its own trigger.

Impact Tremors, Warleader's Call, Purphoros and the pingers (Molten Gatekeeper, Witty Roastmaster, Weftstalker Ardent, Agate Instigator) trigger once per creature, each hitting every opponent. Six tokens with Tremors plus Purphoros is 18 damage to each opponent.

CR 603.2
Purphoros is a creature only with devotion to red of 5 or more.

Devotion counts red mana symbols in the mana costs of permanents you control, including Purphoros' own {R}. Tokens have no mana cost and add nothing. It's indestructible either way.

CR 700.5
Mob Justice and Battle Hymn count on resolution.

Creatures you tapped to pay for Aziza's copy still count. Copying either one counts your creatures again.

CR 608.2h
Indestructible doesn't stop -X/-X.

Moogles' Valor, Grand Crescendo and Boros Charm save your board from destroy wipes (Blasphemous Act, Farewell's destroy mode, Austere Command), but not from Toxic Deluge, the wipe you face most (470 times), which kills by toughness.

CR 704.5f
Skullclamp kills 1/1 tokens on the spot.

+1/-1 drops a 1/1 to 0 toughness, so it dies right away and you draw two. Equip {1} for two cards, as long as you have tokens.

CR 704.5f
Song of Totentanz's Rats can't block.

They still give Impact Tremors triggers on entry, and the haste applies to all your creatures that turn.

Other cards in the list

Jeska's Will needs Aziza on the battlefield to choose both modes.

It checks whether you control a commander when you cast it. If Aziza was removed, you get only one mode.

CR Card text
Forth Eorlingas! makes you the monarch.

If your creatures deal combat damage to a player that turn, you become the monarch and draw an extra card each end step until someone takes it by hitting you.

CR 725
Caretaker's Talent levels only at sorcery speed.

Level 2 copies a token, and level 3 gives tokens +2/+2. Level 1's draw happens once per turn, however many token batches enter.

CR 716.2a
Molten Gatekeeper's unearth is a one-turn return.

It comes back with haste and is exiled at end of turn or if it would leave. Unearth only at sorcery speed.

CR 702.84a
Chrome Mox and Ancient Tomb have real costs.

Chrome Mox needs a nonartifact, nonland card exiled from your hand, or it makes no mana. Ancient Tomb deals 2 to you every time you tap it. The simulator doesn't charge either, so the Speed package's gain is a bit overstated.

What it shows

  • Speed wins: 47.0% against 42.5%, with gains almost everywhere and the biggest in the weak spots: Typal +7.5, Blink +8.4, Aggro +7.2, Landfall +6.7. Getting knocked out first drops from 35% to 31% of tables.
  • About half of that is Guttersnipe (44.7% on its own, +2.2). The rest comes from Ancient Tomb and Chrome Mox, and that part is overstated: the simulator doesn't charge Tomb's 2 damage or Mox's exiled card.
  • Board added little (44.1%, within noise), and combining it with Speed (45.3%) was worse than Speed alone.
  • Three cards are blank to the simulator and played no part in these numbers: Return the Favor, Caretaker's Talent and Wildfire Awakener. They were left in the list, so their real value isn't measured here.

Earlier testing (Oct 3, single-category pods on the classic 4-category gauntlet) compared this list against three rebuilds: Big Army, Ritual Storm and Tremors Drain. All three lost to the EDHREC average list, and Mob Justice for To Arms! was the only single-card swap that clearly helped.

How this was run

  • Opponents are EDHREC average decklists, using both the Upgraded (bracket 3) and Optimized (bracket 4) versions. Each series rolls each opponent's bracket at random and keeps it for all three games.
  • Every card in every deck was tagged from its real rules text (Forge card scripts): mana, removal, wipes, counterspells, stax effects, tokens, anthems, drains, combos and alternate wins. Opponents play the cards they actually draw.
  • Seat order is random each game. A game that hits 25 rounds counts as a draw, which counts as a loss for the series.
  • Limits: decisions are rule-based, not expert play. Politics, deals and bluffs don't exist, and some cards are simplified. Treat the matchup shape as the finding, not the exact percentages.
