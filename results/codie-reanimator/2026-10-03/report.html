Predictions vs results

  • A, weak to Storm, Control and Stax: held. 19.2%, 19.6% and 15.7%, each 5 to 6 points under the baseline.
  • A, interaction would fix the creature matchups: missed. Tokens, Typal and Aggro all got worse. A lands more infinite-combo kills than the baseline (3,757 vs 3,436), but its combat kills fall from 2,594 to 867. The baseline's prepare bodies were winning games in combat, and removal didn't replace that.
  • B, weak to Reanimator: partly held. 24.6%, below its own average but 3 points above the baseline. Opposing Living Death wipes it 151 times.
  • B, weak to Control: missed. 30.1% / 23.0%, B's biggest gain (+6 games). Recursive threats come back after wipes; combo pieces don't.
  • B, weak to Aristocrats: held. 15.9% / 7.3%. Edicts and drains blunt big single threats.
  • Surprise: Lifegain is B's worst category at 5.8% with 0 series won. Amalia Benavides Aguirre wipes the board 347 times in 647 games, which looks like a simulator outlier worth checking.

Swap test on B (300 series, paired shuffles)

  • Etali, Primal Conqueror → Gray Merchant of Asphodel: kept. +2.0 games and +2.3 series overall; +4.9 Turbo combo, +5.3 Enchantress, +4.2 Typal, +3.7 Control; no category worse by 3 or more.
  • Boros Charm → Kozilek's Return: −0.2 overall (+4.6 Typal, −3.3 Storm). Not kept.
  • Heroic Intervention → Ashen Rider: −0.2 overall (+3.9 Typal, −3.9 Artifacts). Not kept.

The baseline was rerun after the engine change because opponents' Animate Dead, Entomb, Faithless Looting and similar cards now work too. That lowered the baseline from 23.6% to 21.4% mean games, with the biggest drops in Turbo combo, Reanimator and Artifacts.

What it shows

  • The combined list wins: 29.7% of games and 22.7% of series, against 25.2% / 17.5% for the previous list. It's now the list in this report.
  • Defensive does what it was built for. Tokens rise from 14.3% to 22.5%, Landfall from 16.5% to 24.7%, and Blink and Aristocrats each gain about 5 points. Codie is removed 18% less often.
  • Tempo mostly helps the matchups that were already fine: Turbo combo +7.7, Storm +7.2, Artifacts +7.2. Getting a fatty down a turn earlier wins races.
  • Typal didn't move (13–16% in every build). The Ur-Dragon, Lathril and Shilgengar win in combat before any of these cards matter. It needs a different answer, likely a cheaper sweeper.
  • Lifegain is still low at 13.4% after the Amalia fix. The losses are now real infinite combos (Liesa, Dina, Oloro, Exquisite Blood), not a simulator bug.
  • Watch Voltron (35.0% to 26.5%) and Reanimator (31.0% to 26.7%) on the combined list. Both drops are larger than the noise, and a likely cause is Exhume handing opponents their best creature back.

Engine changes for this round: Amalia Benavides Aguirre no longer wipes the board when cast (it only does so at exactly 20 power), self-mill stops below 25 cards in the library, and Crippling Fear, Exhume and the Entomb pilot options are now modeled. The "Previous list" column is a rerun on that fixed engine, so the comparison is fair.

How this was run

  • Opponents are EDHREC average decklists, using both the Upgraded (bracket 3) and Optimized (bracket 4) versions. Each series rolls each opponent's bracket at random and keeps it for all three games.
  • Every card in every deck was tagged from its real rules text (Forge card scripts): mana, removal, wipes, counterspells, stax effects, tokens, anthems, drains, combos and alternate wins. Opponents play the cards they actually draw.
  • Seat order is random each game. A game that hits 25 rounds counts as a draw, which counts as a loss for the series.
  • Limits: decisions are rule-based, not expert play. Politics, deals and bluffs don't exist, and some cards are simplified. Treat the matchup shape as the finding, not the exact percentages.
